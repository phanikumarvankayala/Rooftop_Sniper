Split the grid into rectangles

How to play: drag from one square to another to draw a rectangle. Tap a rectangle to remove it.
  • Cover the whole grid with rectangles that don't overlap
  • Each rectangle holds exactly one number, and that number is how many squares it covers
  • A small tag on a number shows its shape: ■ square, ▬ wide, ▮ tall
  • Rectangles turn grey with a dashed edge if they break a rule
