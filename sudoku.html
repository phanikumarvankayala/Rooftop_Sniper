Mini Sudoku

6×6 · fill 1–6 in every row, column and box
⏱ 0:00
How to play: fill every empty square with a number from 1 to 6 so each row, column and 2×3 box has each number exactly once.
  • Hint fills in the selected square, or a random one if none is selected
  • Clear erases all your numbers but keeps the puzzle
  • Reset starts a brand-new puzzle
  • Clashing numbers turn red. On a keyboard, use 1–6, the arrow keys and Backspace
